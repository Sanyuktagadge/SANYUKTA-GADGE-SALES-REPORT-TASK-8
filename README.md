# SANYUKTA-GADGE-SALES-REPORT-TASK-8
## Project Overview ###
This dashboard provides a visual analysis of sales data between 2018 and 2021, offering insights into revenue, profit, quantity, and discount trends. It is designed to support decision-making by identifying key performance areas across different segments, regions, and product categories.

## Dashboard Features
Total Sales Overview: Displays total sales, quantity, profit, and discount.

Sales by Segment: Compares Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

Sales by Category & Sub-Category: Highlights performance across product lines like Technology, Furniture, Phones, Chairs, etc.

Sales by Region and State: Geographical view of performance across West, Central, East, and South regions.

Sales Over Time: Time-series chart showing sales trends by order date.

## Key Insights (2021)
The West region contributed the highest sales, with 31.58% of the total revenue.

The Consumer segment was the leading contributor, accounting for over 50% of sales.

Phones and Chairs were the top-selling sub-categories.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Power BI for dashboard creation

Excel/CSV dataset as the data source

Map visualizations via Bing Maps integration

## How to Use
Open the dashboard in Power BI Desktop or Power BI Service.

Use the year filter (2018–2021) to analyze specific periods.

Hover or click on visuals to drill down into specific segments or regions.

Use this analysis to support product strategy, regional planning, and segment targeting.
